# 03 — The Covenant Group
*Last updated: 2022 story bible, pre-production draft*

---

## OVERVIEW
The Covenant Group is not an organisation. It has no legal existence, no headquarters, no employees, no bank account, no registered name. What it has is a portfolio of relationships — between companies, between governments, between academic institutions — managed the way a conductor manages an orchestra. No single instrument knows the full score.

It was formed in 1994 by seven individuals who shared a worldview, a reading of history, and a patience that most people who want power do not have. Their thesis was simple, cold, and in their own estimation, correct: the most durable form of control is not force. It is dependency. And the most durable form of dependency is one the dependent population actively welcomes.

They have never been wrong about anything that mattered. That is the most important thing to understand about them.

---

## ORIGIN — GENEVA, 1994

The meeting that formed the Covenant Group happened on the evening of October 14th, 1994, in a private dining room of a Geneva hotel whose name does not need to appear in any document because none of the seven would ever write it down. They were attending, or using as cover, a World Health Organisation nutrition policy conference. They were not delegates.

What brought them together was a shared reaction to watching the Soviet Union's collapse play out over the preceding three years. Not horror. Not triumph. Something more clinical — the recognition that an entire civilisation's institutional infrastructure had been dismantled inside a decade, and that the population it served had accepted almost every replacement system offered to it, as long as that system arrived in the language of modernity, efficiency, and improvement.

The lesson they drew was not about Russia. Russia was the proof of concept. The lesson was about the mechanics of institutional replacement at population scale — how it happens, how fast it can happen, and what conditions are required to make a population not merely accept a new dependency but prefer it to the old autonomy.

They called themselves, in private correspondence only, The Covenant Group. The word covenant was chosen deliberately — an agreement to do something together that none could accomplish alone. The name never appeared on any filing, any letterhead, or any public document. It was simply what they called themselves in the eleven letters, spread across four years, that constitute the only written record of the group's formation.

---

## THE THESIS — THE NUTRITION TRANSITION

The Covenant Group's foundational intellectual framework was built around a real and well-documented phenomenon: the global nutrition transition — the shift from traditional whole-food diets toward processed, packaged, and industrially produced food that was already underway across the developing world in the 1990s.

What the seven saw was not a market trend but a structural vulnerability opening up in real time. Food corporations were moving into that space — but food corporations were clumsy, visible, and politically attackable. The Covenant Group wanted to operate one layer deeper. Not in food itself. In the biological need that food fulfils.

Their foundational private paper — never published, never leaked, circulated only among members and never stored digitally — articulated the thesis as follows:

*"Natural food systems are inefficient delivery mechanisms for human nutrition. They are geographically variable, climatically unstable, culturally resistant to standardisation, and impossible to monopolise cleanly. A synthetic nutritional supply chain — precision-engineered supplements delivering calibrated macro and micronutrient profiles — solves all of these problems simultaneously. The transition from natural food dependency to synthetic supplement dependency is not a dystopia. It is an upgrade. The question is only who controls the upgrade."*

This was not, in their minds, malevolent. It was rational. The supplements would work. The health outcomes would be measurable and positive. People would live longer and feel better. The control embedded in the supply chain would be invisible precisely because the product would be genuinely good.

That is the Huxleyan architecture of the whole enterprise. The trap is not a trap because it hurts you. It is a trap because it helps you just enough that you stop looking for the door.

## OPERATIONAL STRUCTURE

The Covenant Group operates across three pillars, none of which know the full shape of the other two.

### PILLAR ONE — AGROCHEM DEGRADATION
The longest-running programme. Involves the systematic weakening of traditional agricultural supply chains in target regions — not through sabotage, but through policy capture.

The mechanism: fund academic research, lobbying arms, and sympathetic policy think tanks that position traditional food systems as unreliable, unscalable, and unsafe while promoting synthetic alternatives as the modern, responsible solution. The research is real. The funding sources are obscured. The conclusions are predetermined.

In South Asia, the target is the intersection of Kerala's spice and rice agriculture with its heavily import-dependent urban population. A region that cannot feed itself from its own land is a region that will accept whatever feeding system is offered. Climate instability, urbanisation pressure, and supply chain disruption are all accelerants. The Covenant Group does not cause these things. It positions itself to benefit from them.

### PILLAR TWO — VISCHEM AND THE DELIVERY PLATFORM
Vischem Integrated BioSystems is the Covenant Group's primary pharmaceutical instrument. Co-opted from 2011 onward through the Vischer Family Trust. Funded through Alivar Lifesciences. Directed toward the development of a lipid nanoparticle nutritional delivery platform disguised as a COVID-19 therapeutic.

The delivery platform is the keystone technology of the entire supplement programme. Without a reliable mechanism for delivering synthetic micronutrients directly to human tissue — bypassing the variable absorption rates of natural food digestion — the supplement transition cannot be completed at population scale. VIB-C19T was the field validation of that platform. The fact that it produced B.1.VIB and killed people across Kerala is, in the Covenant Group's operational ledger, recorded as an acceptable variance event.

### PILLAR THREE — SUPPLEMENT MANUFACTURING AND DISTRIBUTION
Running parallel to Vischem's pharmaceutical operations since the early 2000s, the Covenant Group has assembled a manufacturing and distribution network spread across seven countries under seventeen different corporate identities, capable of producing and delivering synthetic nutritional supplements at a scale sufficient to serve a population of 50 million people.

The network is not secret in any individual component. Each facility, each company, each distribution arrangement is entirely legitimate. What is secret is that they are connected — that seventeen apparently unrelated companies in seven countries are nodes in a single coordinated supply chain waiting for the moment when a target population is dependent enough to receive it.

Kerala was always the intended first market. High literacy, collapsing agricultural self-sufficiency, dense urban population, a pharmacy culture already oriented toward supplement consumption, and — after B.1.VIB — a traumatised immune landscape that creates genuine nutritional deficiency at population scale. The product will arrive as relief. It will be welcomed. It will work.

## THE SEVEN FOUNDERS

### 01 — THE ECONOMIST
**Nationality:** German
**Background:** Former IMF structural adjustment adviser. Spent the 1990s designing shock therapy programmes for post-Soviet economies. Watched the Russian middle class dissolve in three years and felt professional curiosity, not remorse. Retired from the IMF in 2001 and moved into private advisory work for sovereign wealth funds.

**Ideology:** Believes human populations are variables in an equation. Not with cruelty — with the complete detachment of someone who has never been required to think of them any other way. Has never visited any of the countries whose economies his programmes restructured. Does not consider this a gap.

**Role in the Covenant Group:** Provides the macroeconomic architecture — models the dependency transition timelines, identifies target market sequencing, advises on the economic conditions required to make supplement introduction feel like relief rather than imposition.

**In 2022:** 71 years old. Semi-retired. Still attends the group's annual meeting — one evening, one location, rotated annually — and still provides the clearest analytical summaries of any of the seven. Has no emotional investment in any outcome. Finds this clarifying.

---

### 02 — THE AGRONOMIST
**Nationality:** Dutch
**Background:** Agricultural scientist. Holds seventeen patents on synthetic micronutrient compounds. Has spent forty years publishing peer-reviewed research on the limits of natural food systems to sustain projected global population growth. His academic work is entirely legitimate and widely cited.

**Ideology:** Genuinely, completely believes that natural food systems cannot feed the 22nd century and that synthetic nutrition is not a choice but an inevitability. Views people who romanticise traditional food culture the way an engineer views someone who misses horse-drawn carts — not with contempt exactly, but with the mild impatience of someone waiting for the sentiment to pass.

**Role in the Covenant Group:** Provides scientific legitimacy. His published work forms the academic backbone of Pillar One's policy capture programme. When Alivar Lifesciences funds a nutrition research paper that concludes traditional diets are nutritionally deficient, that paper cites his work. He does not write those papers. He does not need to.

**Personal detail:** Has not eaten natural food since 2003. Not as a statement. As a logical conclusion. He eats his own compounds exclusively. His health, by every measurable marker, is excellent.

**In 2022:** 68 years old. Still publishing. Still presenting at conferences. Entirely untroubled.

---

### 03 — THE BANKER
**Nationality:** Luxembourgish
**Background:** Sovereign wealth management. Spent his career constructing financial architectures of extraordinary complexity across multiple jurisdictions — the kind of structures that are entirely legal, entirely opaque, and entirely designed to make money move without being followed.

**Ideology:** None. Genuinely none. Has no opinion on the supplement transition, the nutrition thesis, the geopolitical implications, or the human cost of any of it. Sees the Covenant Group as a long-horizon investment thesis with an unusually patient return timeline. Manages its financial architecture with the same professional detachment he would apply to any other client.

**Role in the Covenant Group:** The money. Alivar Lifesciences, the seventeen supplement distribution companies, the Covenant-connected sovereign wealth positions in the Vischer Family Trust — all of it flows through structures he designed and maintains. If the Covenant Group is an orchestra, he is the venue. Without him, there is nowhere to play.

**Personal detail:** Coaches youth football on weekends. Attends his children's school plays. Is present and engaged as a parent in a way that would surprise anyone who knew what he spent his working hours doing. Completely compartmentalised. Finds no contradiction in any of it.

**In 2022:** 59 years old. At the peak of his professional capability. Has already designed the financial architecture for the supplement distribution rollout in India. The structures are ready. They are waiting for the moment.

---

### 04 — THE INTELLIGENCE MAN
**Nationality:** French
**Background:** Retired from the DGSE — France's external intelligence service — in 2012 after a thirty-year career that included postings in West Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia. The specific nature of his DGSE work is classified. The general nature of it was: make things happen in other countries without leaving French fingerprints.

**Ideology:** Strategic realism, undiluted. Believes the world is organised around power and that the only meaningful question is who holds it. The Covenant Group's supplement agenda is, in his reading, a geopolitical operation — the acquisition of a form of leverage over emerging market populations that no military force and no economic sanction can replicate. He finds it elegant.

**Role in the Covenant Group:** Designed and manages SOLVANE. Provides governmental access — to intelligence networks, to regulatory bodies, to the kind of people who can make a DCGI fast-track approval happen without it looking like anything other than a regulatory decision. The connection between Vischem and unnamed European sovereign partners referenced in the SOLVANE documents runs through him.

**In 2022:** 64 years old. When the Kerala death toll crossed 300, he filed it under acceptable variance and moved on. Has managed darker programmes. Considers SOLVANE, even with B.1.VIB, one of his cleaner operations. The loss of control over the variant's spread is a professional irritation, not a moral crisis. He is already working on the containment narrative.

---

### 05 — THE VISCHER INSIDER
**Nationality:** Swiss
**Background:** Corporate governance and private wealth management. Joined the Vischer Family Trust's board in 2014 through a series of investment relationships so legitimately structured that no audit has ever found cause for concern. Has known Dr. Maren Vischer for eleven years. She considers him a trusted adviser.

**Ideology:** Loyalty to the Covenant Group's thesis, inherited from a previous generation of Swiss industrial money that understood long before 1994 that the most durable power is the kind that looks like service.

**Role in the Covenant Group:** The operational link to Vischem. Manages Dr. Maren Vischer's blind spots with deliberate precision — ensuring she has the information she needs to run the company effectively and none of the information that would cause her to stop. Henrik Saal reports to him before reporting to Maren. Dr. Priya Anand's accelerated career trajectory at Vischem Bengaluru was partly his recommendation.

**The specific skill:** Makes things not happen. Not in a crude way — no documents are destroyed, no emails are deleted. He simply ensures that the questions that would lead somewhere are never quite asked, the connections that would mean something are never quite made. Eleven years of that, applied consistently, is indistinguishable from institutional culture.

**In 2022:** 56 years old. Has had three conversations with Maren Vischer in the weeks following the March incident. In each one he was warm, steady, and completely in control of what she understood to be the situation. She has not yet asked the question he cannot answer. He does not believe she will.

---

### 06 — THE SCIENTIST
**Nationality:** Swiss
**Background:** Biochemist. Designed the lipid nanoparticle nutritional delivery platform that became VIB-C19T. Has been working with Alivar Lifesciences — understanding it as an advanced nutrition research foundation — since 2013. His name appears on twelve of the platform's foundational patents.

**Ideology:** Scientific purity, in the specific sense of someone who is interested in whether something works and entirely uninterested in what it is used for. The platform works. He knows this with complete certainty because he built it. What it delivers — nutrients, peptides, or something else — is, to him, a downstream application question.

**Role in the Covenant Group:** Technical authority. The only person in the group who fully understands the delivery platform at a mechanistic level. When B.1.VIB emerged, he was the first person The Intelligence Man called. His assessment — delivered in a fifteen-minute phone call with no emotional register — was that the mutation was a predictable consequence of the high-dosage cohort's immune suppression profile and that the variant's behaviour would stabilise within a defined parameter range.

He was wrong about the stabilisation. He has not yet acknowledged this.

**Personal detail:** Keeps meticulous lab notes dating to 1987. Every experiment, every result, every anomaly, recorded in handwritten journals stored in a fireproof cabinet in his Basel apartment. The journals for 2020–2022 contain, in precise technical language, a complete record of everything that produced B.1.VIB. He does not consider destroying them. The record is the record. Sleeps without difficulty.

**In 2022:** 61 years old. Following the global spread of B.1.VIB with scientific interest. Treating it as a longitudinal study. Taking notes.

---

### 07 — THE SUCCESSOR
**Nationality:** French-Dutch
**Background:** Inherited her founding seat from her father — one of the original seven — at age 31 when he died of pancreatic cancer in 2019. The others initially underestimated her. This was the last mistake any of them made in her presence.

**Ideology:** Her father's thesis, stripped of his residual sentimentality about it. He occasionally expressed regret — not about the programme, but about the specific humans who would be caught in particular transition events. She does not share this. She considers regret a form of self-indulgence that the seriousness of the work does not permit.

**Role in the Covenant Group:** The acceleration. Every compressed timeline decision from 2019 onward has been hers. She approved SOLVANE's move to high-dosage testing in four minutes. She made the decision to proceed with B.1.VIB data collection even after it became clear the variant was mutating beyond projected parameters. She is the reason the Covenant Group is in Kerala in 2022 instead of 2025.

**Personal detail:** Visited Kochi in 2019 on a scoping trip — one of several target market assessments she conducted personally after taking her seat. Ate at a small restaurant in Fort Kochi. Noted the food culture in her field journal as *"sentimental, economically irrational, and therefore replaceable."* The journal entry is three sentences long. She moved on to the next page.

**In 2022:** 34 years old. The youngest person in the room at every meeting. Always the last to speak. When she does, the decision is made.

## THE COVENANT GROUP AND ASTERISK

Asterisk Incorporated's 2019 lunar prospecting mission found something at the south pole — the specific nature of the finding is not public — that the Covenant Group learned about through The Intelligence Man's networks within six weeks.

What they understood from that finding: if Asterisk's off-world resource programme proceeds at its current pace, and if India — Asterisk's primary emerging-market partner through ISRO's commercial arm — stabilises and develops genuine off-world resource access, then the Covenant Group's Earth-bound supplement dependency programme becomes strategically irrelevant within a generation.

A self-sufficient population with access to off-world resource streams does not need to depend on a terrestrial supplement supply chain. The window was closing. The Successor identified this immediately. The timeline compression that caused SOLVANE to accelerate, that caused the high-dosage cohort to be approved, that caused B.1.VIB to emerge — all of it traces back to one finding on the lunar south pole in 2019.

The Covenant Group does not have a plan for Asterisk. This is the only thing they do not have a plan for. It is the thing that, if anything keeps The Economist awake at night — which nothing does — it would be this.

---

## THE COVENANT GROUP IN THE GAME

The Covenant Group never appears directly in the game. No member is encountered in person. No meeting is observed. They exist entirely in documents — the SOLVANE files, the Alivar procurement records, the fragmentary private correspondence that Siddharth and Zara piece together across the investigation chapters.

This is intentional. An antagonist you never see is more frightening than one you face. The player understands, by the game's final chapter, exactly what the Covenant Group is and what it has done. They do not get to shoot at it. They do not get to confront it. What they get to do is make one crack in something that was built to be seamless.

Whether that crack holds is a question the game does not answer.
