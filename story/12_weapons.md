# Weapons
Weapons in Project Chem Warfare are from three main sub-factions within its universe. 

## Factions
1. Misriah Armory - supplying Indian subcontinent with standard military grade kinetic weapons.
2. Amperdune Tech - supplying private security forces with Laser weapons
3. ADZ Weapons Division - supplying heavy weapons for revolutionaries from the black market
