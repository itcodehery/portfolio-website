# 04 — MERIDIAN
*Last updated: 2022 story bible, pre-production draft*

---

## OVERVIEW
MERIDIAN has no public name. No registered entity. No official existence of any kind. It appears in the world only as a pattern — a series of events that should not be connected but are, a series of people who should not know each other but do, a series of outcomes that should not have been possible but happened anyway.

It is a small, extraordinarily well-resourced covert network that has spent the better part of a decade mapping the Covenant Group's infrastructure — its shell companies, its funding routes, its facility locations, its human assets — and is now, in 2022, in a position to act.

Kerala is not an accident for MERIDIAN. The B.1.VIB outbreak is the first time the Covenant Group has been visible enough to hit. MERIDIAN has been waiting for exactly this kind of moment. They sent Zara.

---

## MOTTO
**acknowledge. overcome.**

Two words. Lowercase. No subject, no object, no ideology declared. Just a methodology. The motto does not explain what is being acknowledged or what is being overcome. It does not need to. Everyone inside MERIDIAN already knows. Everyone outside MERIDIAN was never going to be told.

---

## ORIGIN — THE LATE 2000s

MERIDIAN did not form in response to the Covenant Group specifically. It formed in response to a pattern that several of its founding members had independently identified across different professional contexts — intelligence, finance, academic research, pharmaceutical regulation — and that none of them could act on alone.

The pattern: a set of decisions being made about the future of human nutrition, human dependency, and human autonomy that were not being made by governments, not being made by markets, and not being made by any institution subject to democratic accountability. They were being made by a small number of private individuals whose financial and operational connections were almost impossible to trace and whose agenda, when you finally mapped it, was something no public institution had the framework to address.

MERIDIAN's founding members were not idealists. They were not revolutionaries in any traditional sense. They were people who had looked at the pattern clearly and concluded that the only entity capable of countering a private network operating outside institutional frameworks was another private network operating outside institutional frameworks.

They were right. They also knew exactly what that meant about themselves.

---

## WHAT MERIDIAN IS NOT

Understanding MERIDIAN requires clearing away what it is not:

**It is not a government operation.** No government funds it, directs it, or knows the full scope of its activity. Several governments have individuals inside their structures who are aware of MERIDIAN's existence and choose not to pursue it. That is not the same as sponsorship.

**It is not a humanitarian organisation.** MERIDIAN does not issue reports. It does not hold press conferences. It does not pursue justice through institutions it has already concluded are compromised. It acts.

**It is not a revolutionary cell.** It has no manifesto, no political programme, no vision of a post-Covenant Group world order that it is trying to install. Its objective is the destruction of a specific network's operational capability. What comes after is not its department.

**It is not on your side.** This is the most important thing to understand about MERIDIAN and about Zara. MERIDIAN's goals align with the protagonists' goals in 2022, in Kerala, in the context of stopping the Covenant Group's supplement rollout. That alignment is tactical, not philosophical. MERIDIAN would sacrifice Arjun or Siddharth without hesitation if the operational calculus required it. Zara knows this. She has not told them.

---

## THE ASTERISK CONNECTION

MERIDIAN's relationship to Asterisk Incorporated is the most significant unanswered question in the game's lore. It is never confirmed. It is never denied. The following is what can be inferred from the evidence available in the game's documents and environmental storytelling:

MERIDIAN's funding is substantial. The resources required to maintain a decade-long intelligence operation mapping the Covenant Group's infrastructure — across multiple jurisdictions, with operatives in multiple countries — are not available to a small group of concerned individuals operating on personal savings. The money comes from somewhere.

Asterisk Incorporated is the entity with the most direct strategic interest in the Covenant Group's failure. A world in which the Covenant Group successfully transitions major emerging-market populations onto synthetic supplement dependency is a world that becomes progressively less interested in off-world resource development — you do not need to reach for the stars if you are comfortable where you are. Asterisk's entire long-term business model depends on human populations remaining curious, autonomous, and hungry for more than what they have been given.

The Successor knows about the Asterisk connection. She does not know the full scope of it. This is the one piece of intelligence MERIDIAN has successfully kept from the Covenant Group. It is also, in The Successor's private assessment, the thing most likely to eventually destroy her.

---

## THE BULL

MERIDIAN's ghost symbol — a bull or bison, seen only as a dark watermark behind the organisation's name in its rare internal documents — was chosen by the founding members for a specific reason.

The bull is not a symbol of power in the conventional sense. It is a symbol of something that cannot be reasoned with, redirected, or negotiated with once it is moving. You can get out of its way. You can try to stop it. You cannot change its direction by argument.

MERIDIAN is not interested in argument. It mapped the Covenant Group for a decade. It identified the moment. It sent Zara. The bull is already moving.

The secondary reading — which some MERIDIAN members acknowledge and others consider overly literary — is that the bull is also the thing that gets sacrificed. MERIDIAN's operatives do not expect to survive the operations they run at full commitment. Zara knows this too. It has not changed anything she does.

## MERIDIAN'S INTELLIGENCE ON THE COVENANT GROUP

By 2022, MERIDIAN has mapped the following with high confidence:
- All seventeen supplement distribution companies in the Covenant Group's manufacturing network, across seven countries
- The Alivar Lifesciences funding structure and its connections to Vischem's research allocation
- The identities of six of the seven Covenant Group founders — The Successor's identity was confirmed only in 2021
- The existence of SOLVANE, though not its full technical specifications
- The Vischer Insider's role on the Vischer Family Trust board
- The location and operational purpose of the Vischem Kochi port facility

MERIDIAN does not have:
- The full technical specifications of the VIB-C19T platform and its nutritional delivery application
- The contents of The Scientist's lab journals
- Confirmation of what Asterisk found on the lunar south pole
- The Covenant Group's complete target market sequencing beyond India

The documents that fill these gaps are inside the Vischem Kochi facility. That is why Zara is in Kerala. That is what the game is about.

## MERIDIAN IN THE GAME

### Presence
MERIDIAN is felt before it is named. In the game's early chapters, the player encounters signs of prior intelligence work — a document already flagged, a location already scouted, a contact who received a message they did not expect — without understanding their source. By the midpoint, when Zara's affiliation becomes clear, the player can look back and see MERIDIAN's fingerprints on things that seemed coincidental at the time.

### Communications
MERIDIAN communicates with Zara through a series of dead drops, encrypted text fragments, and pre-arranged signals that the player encounters during her chapters. The communications are always sparse — operational, never explanatory. MERIDIAN does not tell Zara why. It tells her what and where.

### The Question the Game Leaves Open
MERIDIAN's endgame is never disclosed. Destroying the Covenant Group's Kerala operation is the immediate objective. What MERIDIAN intends to do with the complete SOLVANE documentation, the Covenant Group's full network map, and the evidence of the supplement conspiracy — publish it, leverage it, use it as the foundation for something else entirely — the game does not say.

Zara may know. If she does, she has decided the other two do not need to.

## DESIGN LANGUAGE

### Colour
Deep crimson graduating to near-black. The colour of something that has been operational for a long time and has the history to prove it. Not the bright red of ideology or urgency — the dark, dried red of something patient and permanent.

### Typography
The MERIDIAN wordmark runs vertically along the left edge of its documents, separated from the content by a single thin rule. Spaced tracking, military intelligence weight — the typography of classified document spines, NATO designations, signals corps filing systems. It does not announce itself. It identifies itself, precisely, to those who need to know.

### The Ghost Image
The bull watermark sits behind the text at low opacity — dark enough to almost disappear against the near-black background, present enough that once seen it cannot be unseen. This is intentional and exactly correct for MERIDIAN's operational philosophy. They are there. They have always been there. You simply were not looking in the right way.

### Overall Feel
Where Vischem is cold and certain and Asterisk is warm and approachable, MERIDIAN is dark and patient and half-hidden. The three design languages together tell the player everything about the power structure of the world before a word of story is read. Vischem has already won. Asterisk is pretending to be benign. MERIDIAN does not care whether you see it coming.

## ZARA — SUMMARY
*(Full character document: 11_zara.md)*

Zara is MERIDIAN's operative in Kerala. Her cover identity — ex-Vischem contract researcher, Bengaluru facility, left after the March 2022 suspension — is constructed well enough to survive standard verification. It does not survive the kind of scrutiny Arjun applies to it, which is why he does not trust her for the first half of the game.

She was sent to Kerala because MERIDIAN identified the B.1.VIB outbreak as the first occasion on which the Covenant Group's operational infrastructure had been sufficiently exposed to be targeted directly. She was also sent because she is the best operative MERIDIAN has for this kind of work — the kind that requires being trusted by people who have every reason not to trust you, in a situation where being wrong about that trust gets people killed.

She has run four operations of comparable complexity before Kerala. The player never learns what happened in any of them. Zara has not volunteered this information. She will not.
