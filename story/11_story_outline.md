# World Outline
- Within a collapsing Kerala, the Government has turned to private funding to keep up the costs and as a favor, the government has released restrictions on few of the medical research procedures for these companies.
- Mainly, Vischem Integrated Biosystems is at the forefront in bio-medical research with aid in infrastructure from Asterisk Incorporated.
- Vischem is working on the cure for the mutated strain of COV-19 which started in 2023 and has blown up terribly into a worldwide crisis and has collapsed world governments.
- The current year is 2041 where the mutated COV-19 strains are still prevalent and the world has been put into a global martial law.
- Secret people-led organizations are training and hiring mercenaries to hoard food for their people and are living hidden to escape the government's oppressive rule.
- People are being forced to undergo army training and be subjects for experiments conducted from their end. To combat this, people live hidden.
- Companies like Vischem have absolute control over the food economy as fabricated food became the primary source of rations for the population since the last decade.
- Due to the early war, many artifacts and earthly resources that they could have used for the current research are on the brink of exhaustion.
- Asterisk partners with Vischem and SOLVANE to develop a secret technology that would enable a person to navigate and infil through time and extract safely.
- After this tool was invented, after an initial internal crisis, the decision was made to hire mercenaries from one of those people-led organizations called MERIDIAN to carry out these time-heists.
- The first mission for our protagonist is the third time they have sent a merc through time to retrieve an artifact from the past for them.
- These artifacts should help in developing a cure for the worldwide crisis.

# Level 1 Outline
- Takes place in the Space Elevator Colony of Aravinda-1, Kochi, Kerala in 2023.
- Mission Goal: Retrieve specific 'Cryo Tubes' from the Cargo Bay of Aravinda.
- Mission Goal is informed to the player through Aadharsh in the intro cutscene with a camera overview over the whole facility.
- Infil and Extract using Wrist-Mounted Time Navigator (Secret Asterisk Tech).
- Start by player spawning in a landing platform in the Waste Processing Wing of Aravinda-1. (Force player to use Air Grapple)
- Player moves to Main Quadrants Common Area (with empty shops, mini-buildings, etc. Think of Starfield)
- Main Buildings: Pantry, Security, Office Spaces. (No Man's Sky, Ship Area)
- Player fights off security detail as they are here on a cargo run to protect cargo. There are no civilians on board.
- Find Security Building, fight through floors with human soldiers and turrets. Picks up Kinetic weapons in armory room.
- Reach top floor of Security Building, hack main security terminal using Aadharsh. Hack unlocks all 3 quadrants. Triggers drone attack outside building. 
- Player fights more soldiers from Quadrant B.
- In Quadrant B, there has been a thermal coolant leak which has filled the floor which Aadharsh informs the player about. (The floor is lava, Halo Infinite Warship Gbraakon). Player fights drones while going across moving platforms to reach the Quadrant D.
- Player moves directly from Quadrant B to Quadrant D. Passes through server room before reaching quadrant D. Picks up energy weapons in Server Room.
- Player reaches main power plant which is the ring of huge battery wires surrounding the central hole of the elevator capsule. 
- Disable generator battery by EMP Blast (introduce EMP grenades)
- Backup power kicks in, but there is not enough power to move the capsule, so it is stuck. (which is good for us)
- Reach Cargo Station #3 (which is a massive room, should almost trigger Megalophobia). Aadharsh informs player that the artifact to extract should be in Cargo Pod #74.
- Find Cargo Pod #74 while fighting turrets, drones, and crawlers. (Atomic Heart Inspo) (New Enemy - Crawlers: Spider Drones)
- Cutscene with player removing the cryo tubes from the Cargo Pod before getting assaulted again by drones.
- Final drones + soldiers fight. Player needs to lockdown cargo bay to prevent more soldiers to come in.
- After lockdown is triggered, the final cutscene plays with the player extracting successfully. Aadharsh gives a countdown for the extraction.
- Cue the title screen and on to new level.
