# 01 — Master Timeline
*Last updated: 2022 story bible, pre-production draft*

---

## PRE-HISTORY — THE FOUNDATIONS (1987–2009)

**1987**
— Ernst Vischer, polymer chemist, founds Vischem GmbH in Basel, Switzerland. First products: medical-grade tubing, bioreactor seals, dialysis membranes. Genuine scientific idealism. No political agenda.

**1991**
— Soviet Union collapses. Western economists and investment bankers pour into Moscow to advise on privatisation. A small number of them watch the process closely and draw a different conclusion than most — not about Russia, but about the mechanics of institutional collapse and population dependency.

**1994**
— Seven individuals meet informally in Geneva under cover of a WHO nutrition policy conference. They are not delegates. The meeting lasts one evening. They call themselves, in private correspondence only, **The Covenant Group**. No legal entity is formed. No minutes are kept.

**Late 1990s**
— Vischem pivots into biochemical compounds. Renames to Vischem Integrated BioSystems. Begins supplying pharmaceutical-grade polymer substrates and microfluidic diagnostic components to major pharmaceutical manufacturers globally. Becomes quietly essential to multiple supply chains.

**1999–2000**
— The Covenant Group circulates its foundational private paper — never published, never leaked — outlining the nutrition transition thesis: synthetic supplement dependency as the endpoint of a controlled, patient, multi-decade programme targeting emerging market populations.

**2000s**
— Covenant Group members spend the decade building positions — in pharmaceutical investment, agricultural commodity trading, European sovereign wealth management, and intelligence-adjacent policy advisory roles. No operational activity. Pure infrastructure building.

---

## THE CO-OPTION DECADE (2010–2019)

**2010**
— Space exploration acceleration begins in earnest. Private capital enters launch and resource extraction. What will become Asterisk Incorporated begins as a Luxembourg-registered consortium of materials scientists and sovereign wealth managers.

**2011**
— The Covenant Group identifies Vischem Integrated BioSystems as an ideal vehicle for its delivery platform programme. The Vischer Family Trust's investment relationships are quietly entangled with Covenant-connected sovereign wealth vehicles in Luxembourg and the Netherlands. The Vischer Insider — a board member of the Trust — becomes the operational link.

**2013**
— Alivar Lifesciences registered in Luxembourg. Described publicly as a charitable nutrition research foundation. Actually a Covenant Group funding instrument. Begins directing an annual research allocation to Vischem's advanced biomaterials division.

**2014–2016**
— Vischem's lipid nanoparticle delivery platform — the technology that will eventually become VIB-C19T — is developed in this period. Original purpose: a nutritional payload delivery mechanism designed to deliver synthetic micronutrient profiles directly to gut and lung tissue. Not a drug. Not a vaccine. A feeding system.

**2016**
— Vischem establishes its Bengaluru facility on Hosur Road. Officially: Asia-Pacific expansion for manufacturing microfluidic diagnostic components. Actually: the chosen site for live-environment platform testing once conditions are right. Employs 1,400 people.

— Asterisk Incorporated formally incorporated in Luxembourg. Begins first prospecting missions to near-Earth asteroids.

**2018**
— Project SOLVANE initiated — a classified dual-use programme formalised between Vischem's advanced biomaterials division and unnamed sovereign partners, via The Intelligence Man (retired DGSE). Objective: engineer a lipid nanoparticle platform that delivers immunosuppressive peptide sequences indistinguishably from a legitimate therapeutic compound in any standard regulatory audit.

— Dr. Maren Vischer, granddaughter of founder Ernst Vischer, appointed CEO. Brilliant, genuine, entirely unaware of SOLVANE or the Covenant Group's co-option of the company. Considered an asset precisely because of her sincerity.

**2019**
— Asterisk completes its first crewed lunar prospecting mission. Finds something at the lunar south pole. The finding is classified internally and not disclosed to any government partner. The Covenant Group, through The Intelligence Man's networks, learns what was found within six weeks.

— The Covenant Group's original 15–20 year supplement dependency timeline is immediately compressed. SOLVANE accelerates.

— MERIDIAN — a small covert network with no public name — begins active surveillance of the Covenant Group's infrastructure. Origin and funding unknown. First MERIDIAN operative identified in proximity to a Covenant Group asset: Geneva.

## THE PANDEMIC YEARS (2020–2021)

**Early 2020**
— COVID-19 pandemic begins. For the Covenant Group, this is an accelerant — it provides legitimate cover for emergency pharmaceutical development, compressed regulatory timelines, and a global population primed to accept novel therapeutic interventions.

— SOLVANE and VIB-C19T formally merge into a single programme. Two sets of objectives. Two sets of funders. Two sets of expectations about what success means.

**Mid 2020**
— Vischem Bengaluru facility's BSL-2 certified research wing quietly upgraded. Work on VIB-C19T begins in earnest.

**February 2021**
— Drug Controller General of India grants Vischem conditional development clearance for VIB-C19T under India's fast-track COVID provisions. Announced February 11th. Covered briefly. PharmWatch India raises concerns immediately. Nobody listens.

— Kerala state government, under pressure from the Omicron wave, is among the most supportive state voices for accelerated therapeutic development.

**April–December 2021**
— Phase I and early Phase II trials of VIB-C19T conducted across Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad. Results are good. Too good. Vischem's Bengaluru team pushes internally for a high-dosage cohort to achieve complete cytokine suppression rather than mere mitigation.

— India's Vaccine Maitri programme peaks. India positions itself as the world's pharmaceutical supplier. The Covenant Group watches this with focused attention. The window is closing faster than even their compressed timeline anticipated.

## THE CRISIS YEAR (2022)

**January 2022**
— DCGI approves Vischem's high-dosage cohort, pressured by the ongoing Omicron wave. The Vischer Insider ensures the approval is not delayed.

**March 3, 2022**
— **THE INCIDENT.** Twelve high-dosage cohort participants at St. John's Medical College, Bengaluru begin exhibiting acute atypical respiratory symptoms. By evening, hospital infection control escalates to Karnataka Health Department. East Wing sealed at 9:47 PM. Several contract and agency staff — including one nurse from Ernakulam — leave the building in the window before the lockdown is formalised.

**March 4, 2022**
— DCGI issues emergency suspension of VIB-C19T across all three trial sites. Vischem issues a carefully worded statement of cooperation. Two trial participants are dead. Nine are hospitalised.

**March 14, 2022**
— INSACOG upgrades the novel variant — internally designated B.1.VIB — to Variant Under Investigation status. Recommends enhanced surveillance of all St. John's staff including contract workers. Karnataka health department does not complete cross-referencing of agency employment records for seventeen more days.

**March 19, 2022**
— The Wire publishes first major investigative piece. The science behind VIB-C19T's failure begins to become public. Three Vischem research staff resign or go on leave. Vischem contract with Indian government formally suspended.

**March 29, 2022**
— The Ernakulam nurse arrives home by train. She is pre-symptomatic.

**April 2, 2022**
— B.1.VIB confirmed in three patients at Government Medical College Hospital, Ernakulam. Kerala Health Department activates emergency protocol. First community-level exposure to the variant anywhere in the world. Karnataka's containment narrative collapses publicly.

**April–May 2022**
— Containment zone established in Ernakulam. Spreads prove faster than standard COVID-19 protocols can manage. B.1.VIB's symptom profile — deceptively mild for 72 hours, then abrupt deterioration — makes community detection and isolation extremely difficult.

— Sri Lanka's sovereign debt default (April 2022). Colombo Port operations severely degraded. 30% of India's container transshipment traffic disrupted. Pharmaceutical supply chains across South India begin showing dangerous gaps — ventilator-associated sedatives, broad-spectrum antivirals, IV immunosuppressants all affected.

**June 2022**
— Kerala declares State Public Health Emergency. B.1.VIB cases cross 4,200 across seven districts. ICU occupancy above 87% in Ernakulam and Thrissur.

— Tamil Nadu implements heightened health screening at all road and rail entry points from Kerala. De facto border restriction.

— Three suspected B.1.VIB cases identified in Mysuru. Karnataka government begins internal discussions about emergency health powers and potential curfew in Bengaluru.

— Vischem, contract suspended, remains in quiet back-channel contact with Union Health Ministry. Described by sources as "strategic contrition." They are the only organisation that fully understands B.1.VIB's mechanism.

— Deputy Secretary Siddharth Menon, Kerala Education Department, receives a misdirected procurement document showing payment flows from Alivar Lifesciences to an EdTech vendor being fast-tracked into Kerala's school infrastructure programme. He sits on it for three weeks.

**July 2022**
— Employment Division Director Sreekumar Nair's faction completes its deprioritisation of the unemployment welfare disbursal system, introducing new documentation requirements that effectively throttle claims from displaced Vischem contract workers returning to Kerala. A private "redeployment partnership" with Vischem's HR operations is being quietly negotiated.

— Additional Secretary Govind Pillai's Education Department restructuring programme uses ongoing school closures as justification to redirect infrastructure grants toward Alivar-connected EdTech vendors. Child dropout rates begin climbing in containment zone districts.

**August 2022**
— The Wire publishes leaked SOLVANE documents. Swiss Federal Intelligence Service declines comment. DGSE does not respond. Vischem calls documents "fabricated or selectively misrepresented." Union Health Ministry does not comment. PharmWatch India files with National Human Rights Commission.

— MERIDIAN operative Zara arrives in Varkala under cover identity as ex-Vischem contract researcher. Has been tracking the Covenant Group's Kerala operation since April. Has identified both Arjun Nair and Siddharth Menon as potential assets.

## BEYOND THE GAME — THE WIDER WORLD (2022 ONWARD)

*These events form the backdrop to the game's world and inform dialogue, environmental storytelling, and loading screen news fragments. They are not directly playable.*

— B.1.VIB spreads beyond Kerala through Gulf migrant worker travel corridors into the Middle East. Detected in Dubai, Muscat, Riyadh by September 2022.

— Southeast Asian spread follows disrupted Colombo port shipping routes. Bangkok, Colombo, Singapore report clusters by October 2022.

— Russian regional hospitals — chronically underfunded, infrastructurally fragile — begin reporting B.1.VIB cases in Siberia and the Urals by late 2022. No institutional response capacity sufficient to contain it.

— China, running its own parallel population dependency programme through state-directed supplement infrastructure, is not immune. B.1.VIB does not respect competing agendas.

— Asterisk Incorporated announces its second crewed lunar mission. What they found at the south pole has still not been publicly disclosed. The Covenant Group's timeline continues to compress.

— The Covenant Group did not plan a global pandemic. They planned a controlled regional stress test. The fire they started in one room has consumed the building. When the smoke clears, they are still the only ones holding the supplements. That was always going to be true. They just didn't expect to be standing in rubble when it happened.
