# 00 — World Overview

---

## TITLE (WORKING)
**MERIDIAN**

---

## LOGLINE
A first-person shooter set in a collapsing Kerala, 2022 — where a suspended cop, a frightened bureaucrat, and a revolutionary with no name uncover a conspiracy that was never meant to be stopped.

---

## TONE
Mid-level dystopia. Think Huxley, not Orwell. The horror is not violence or surveillance — it is comfort. The trap is something that genuinely helps you, until the moment you realise you cannot live without it. Nobody in power is incompetent. Nobody is cartoonishly evil. Everyone is doing something they believe is rational, necessary, and correct. That is what makes it irreversible.

Gritty. Bleak. No redemption arcs for the antagonists. No whistleblowers. No last-minute change of heart. The protagonists win — if they win — not because the world becomes good but because they refuse to stop moving inside a world that has decided to stay bad.

---

## SETTING
**Kerala, India. 2022.**

A state in crisis on multiple simultaneous fronts:

- A novel SARS-CoV-2 variant — B.1.VIB — spreading from a collapsed pharmaceutical trial in Bengaluru, now a full public health emergency across seven Kerala districts
- Sri Lanka's sovereign debt default disrupting Colombo port operations, breaking pharmaceutical supply chains across South India
- Tamil Nadu imposing de facto border restrictions on Kerala
- Karnataka considering martial law in Bengaluru
- Three Kerala state government departments — Health, Education, and Employment — collapsing from within, each hollowed out by ideologically motivated corruption
- A global space expansion race running in the background, with small research colonies established on the Moon and Mars, and a materials extraction megacorp called Asterisk Incorporated positioned at the centre of off-world resource geopolitics

---

## THE FOUR FACTIONS

**VISCHEM INTEGRATED BIOSYSTEMS**
A Swiss biomedical component manufacturer, founded 1987 in Basel. Publicly respected, politically invisible, technically essential to global pharmaceutical supply chains. Secretly co-opted from 2011 onward by the Covenant Group. Their COVID-19 therapeutic trial in Bengaluru — VIB-C19T — was never solely a medical programme. It was a field test for a nutritional payload delivery platform, and its catastrophic failure produced B.1.VIB. Design language: monochrome black and white, cold, institutional, permanent.

**THE COVENANT GROUP**
Seven Western European individuals — economists, bankers, scientists, intelligence operatives — who formed an informal arrangement in Geneva in 1994 with a single long-horizon thesis: transition global populations from natural food dependency to synthetic supplement dependency, and control the supply chain. No legal existence. No headquarters. Operates entirely through relationships between companies, governments, and academic institutions. The Huxleyan antagonist — they are not trying to hurt anyone. They are trying to make everyone comfortable enough to stop asking questions.

**MERIDIAN**
A small, extremely well-resourced covert network with no public name and no public existence. Has spent a decade mapping the Covenant Group's infrastructure and is now, in 2022, in a position to act. Their relationship to Asterisk Incorporated is implied but never confirmed. Their endgame is never fully disclosed. Design language: deep crimson and black, vertical classified-document typography, a ghost bull. Motto: *acknowledge. overcome.*

**ASTERISK INCORPORATED**
A Luxembourg-incorporated materials and resource extraction megacorp — the SpaceX of off-world mining. Publicly traded, majority owned by a consortium of American, Japanese, and Indian sovereign wealth vehicles. Has operational partnerships with ISRO's commercial arm. Has found something on or near the lunar south pole that the Covenant Group knows about and the public does not. Design language: warm blue gradient, approachable, optimistic — the visual language of something pretending to be benign.

---

## THE THREE PROTAGONISTS

**ARJUN NAIR** — 34, ex-Indian Army, Kerala Police Special Branch, suspended. Varkala. Primary combat protagonist. Does not trust institutions but cannot walk away from something wrong. Carries a service pistol he never handed back.

**SIDDHARTH MENON** — 41, IAS officer, Kerala Education Department, Thiruvananthapuram posted to Ernakulam. Primary investigation protagonist. Believes in systems even when they are broken. Has a document he was never meant to see and has spent three weeks deciding what to do about it.

**ZARA** — late 20s, no surname, no verified origin. Poses as an ex-Vischem contract researcher. Is not. MERIDIAN operative, sent to Kerala specifically because the B.1.VIB outbreak has made the Covenant Group visible for the first time. Primary stealth protagonist. Always three steps ahead. Her endgame is her own.

---

## THE CENTRAL CONSPIRACY — ONE PARAGRAPH
Vischem's COVID-19 therapeutic trial in Bengaluru was secretly a field test for a nutritional payload delivery platform designed by the Covenant Group — a cabal of seven Western Europeans racing to transition global populations onto synthetic supplement dependency before Asterisk Incorporated's off-world resource programme gives emerging nations like India the autonomy to make that dependency impossible. The trial was accelerated recklessly to meet the Covenant Group's compressed timeline, producing a novel viral variant — B.1.VIB — that escaped containment, reached Kerala through a single administrative failure, and triggered a cascading regional crisis that the Covenant Group did not plan but are now exploiting anyway. Three people — a suspended cop, a frightened bureaucrat, and a revolutionary with no name — are the only ones in a position to stop it. None of them were supposed to be.

---

## GAME FORMAT
- Engine: Godot Engine, first-person 3D shooter
- Structure: Linear, cinematic — story-driven like Half-Life
- Chapter structure: Interleaved — chapters alternate between the three protagonists
- Gameplay modes: Gunfights against Vischem paramilitary/private security, stealth infiltration of Vischem facilities, investigation through hacking and document hunting
- Setting locations: Varkala (opening), Ernakulam/Kochi (primary), Thiruvananthapuram (Siddharth's sections), Vischem Kochi port facility (climax)

---

## WHAT THE STORY IS REALLY ABOUT
Control disguised as care. The slow replacement of everything messy, unreliable, and human — food, education, employment, medicine — with something cleaner, more efficient, and owned by someone else. The protagonists do not save the world. They make a crack in something that was supposed to be seamless. Whether that crack holds is a question the game does not answer.
